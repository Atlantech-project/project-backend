## 1. What is asked

Review the migrations of the tables in class diagrams 2C (requests, BAPLIE, Part 1), 2D (Part 2, recommendations, plans, notifications) and 3 (execution and learning), including the tables the AI writes, against the database design: names, types, keys and relations. Fix what does not match. The roadmap's "done when" is "the reviewed group is merged **and the AI Team agrees with the tables it uses**" (`roadmap/roadmap-tasks.json:516-524`).

The review was done during planning (2026-10-28): the generated `schema.sql` (same commit as `develop` `6b76e0e`) compared with `PortSync.sql`, the DDL fixes, Reports A/B, the edit sheet, FCM, CT-09 to CT-17, `workers.sql` and the three diagrams; FK names confirmed on the live database. Result: 44 of 46 tables have no difference; the findings are in section 3.

**Tables in scope (46):**

- 2C (20): `vessel_types`, `vessels`, `berthing_requests`, `berthing_request_cargos`, `berthing_request_status_histories`, `external_systems`, `baplie_files`, `baplie_parse_issues`, `container_manifest_entries`, `forecast_runs`, `environment_forecast_points`, `tide_constituent_sets`, `tide_constituents`, `tide_prediction_runs`, `tide_prediction_points`, `entry_window_proposals`, `feasible_windows`, `blocked_periods`, `demand_forecast_slots`, `part1_runs` (drawn, missing from the scope line).
- 2D (16): `rationale_templates`, `objective_terms`, `solver_runs`, `solver_run_model_versions`, `recommendations`, `recommendation_options`, `recommendation_option_tugs`, `recommendation_overrides`, `recommendation_rationale_factors`, `recommendation_reviews` (drawn, missing from the scope line), `terminal_recommendations`, `storage_allocation_items`, `yard_placement_items`, `operational_plans`, `plan_components`, `notification_logs`.
- 3 (8): `ml_models`, `ml_model_versions`, `actual_status_updates`, `status_types`, `deviation_logs`, `drift_measurements`, `evaluation_metrics`, `retraining_runs`.
- In no diagram (2): `yard_runs` (CT-11), `baplie_parse_runs` (CT-12). Section 8 Q1.

With P1-B2's 45 tables and `license_states`, this covers all 92 contract tables.

**Done when** (each line is checkable by a test or a demo):

**Out of scope:** CHECK and generated-column tests beyond what this task changes (P1-B4) · models, PHP enums and generating `status-vocabulary.json` (P1-B5, CT-21) · seed rows (P1-B6) · the Open decisions DEC-07 (proposal when no window exists, Sprint 5), DEC-09, DEC-11, DEC-24/25, DEC-27, DEC-29, DEC-31, DEC-38, DEC-43, DEC-44: no column change is needed for them now · the sweeper's timeout behaviour (the task that writes the sweeper; Q5 records the rule) · enforcing "the worker fills NULL `vessels` columns only" (the C-06 handler; MySQL column grants cannot express it).
